DATASETS

Different motion. Different challenges.

Five complementary domains for learning how visual–inertial systems behave. Dataset descriptions refer to the official releases, not to a claim that every stream or sequence is included in VIOBench.

Indoor flight

EuRoC MAV

Machine-hall and motion-capture flights with camera–IMU calibration and motion ground truth.

Sensors: Stereo monochrome cameras and synchronized IMU

Learning focus. Start with calibrated visual–inertial data and inspect how lighting and motion affect tracking.

Vicon provides pose ground truth in the motion-capture sequences; Leica supplies position and structure measurements. Read sequence-specific ground-truth details.

  • Official dataset & downloads
  • Dataset paper
Aggressive flight

UZH-FPV

Fast racing laps and free-form indoor and outdoor flights with large rotations and accelerations.

Sensors: Camera and IMU streams; additional event-camera and FPV image data

Learning focus. Explore the effect of rapid image motion and inertial excitation on visual–inertial estimation.

Sensor availability and ground-truth files vary by sequence release; use the official sequence documentation.

  • Official dataset & downloads
  • Dataset paper
Underwater

AQUALOC

Near-seabed harbor and archaeological-site recordings from underwater vehicles.

Sensors: Monocular monochrome camera, MEMS IMU, and pressure sensor

Learning focus. Study visual tracking when texture and illumination are limited, and identify which sensors an estimator actually uses.

A pressure stream is part of the dataset; its availability does not imply that a VIO configuration uses it.

  • Official dataset & downloads
  • Dataset paper
Egocentric motion

LaMAria

Wearable recordings spanning controlled motion and long city-scale trajectories, with surveyed control points and dense pseudo ground truth.

Sensors: Two grayscale cameras and an IMU for SLAM; additional sensors in raw VRS recordings

Learning focus. Connect head motion, image conversion, sensor calibration, and the choice of evaluation reference.

Original VRS and converted ASL/ROS1 images use different camera models. Dense pseudo ground truth and surveyed sparse control points have different evaluation roles.

  • Official dataset & downloads
  • Dataset paper
Legged robotics

GrandTour

Quadruped recordings across indoor and outdoor environments, including varied terrain, illumination, and weather.

Sensors: Cameras, IMUs, LiDAR, depth, proprioception, and GNSS

Learning focus. Explore calibration, synchronization, and sensor selection in a multimodal robot dataset.

The full dataset is multimodal. A camera–IMU experiment uses only a declared subset of the available streams; exact ground-truth availability is mission-specific.

  • Official dataset & downloads
  • Dataset paper

Choose the streams your experiment actually uses

A multimodal dataset may include LiDAR, pressure, events, GNSS, or other measurements. Their presence does not mean a camera–IMU estimator uses them. Read sequence-specific calibration and ground-truth notes before evaluation.
