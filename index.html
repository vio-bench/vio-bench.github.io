A Cross-Domain Benchmark of Visual–Inertial Odometry

  • 11VIO systems
  • 5Public datasets
  • 98Selected sequences

Abstract

Visual–inertial odometry (VIO) provides metric six-degree-of-freedom (6-DoF) ego-motion from onboard cameras and inertial sensors. Existing evaluations, however, remain difficult to directly compare across domains and computing platforms. We present VIOverse, which to our knowledge is the first VIO-specific study to systematically compare 11 open-source VIO systems under a common evaluation framework across five public datasets spanning indoor micro-aerial flight, aggressive flight, underwater operation, wearable AR, and legged robotics. We additionally profile processing time, CPU utilization, and process memory on desktop and embedded platforms. Together, this cross-domain and cross-platform benchmark exposes limitations that are easily missed by isolated accuracy or timing comparisons and provides practical guidance for VIO evaluation and development. In particular, trajectory error should be interpreted together with valid-path coverage, metric scale should be preserved under consistent alignment, and computational performance should be evaluated on the target platform. We fully open-source VIOverse on our project website, along with tutorials and complete results, providing a reproducible and extensible foundation for VIO research. Building on top of VIO (ego-motion estimation), we also provide a broader vision of egocentric intelligence.
