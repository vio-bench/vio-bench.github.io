A Cross-Domain Benchmark of Visual–Inertial Odometry

The VIOverse benchmark

We benchmark 11 open-source visual–inertial odometry (VIO) systems spanning direct, KLT-based, descriptor-based, and learned visual processing, with filtering- and optimization-based estimators.

  • ROVIO
  • SVO Pro
  • VINS-Fusion
  • BASALT
  • OpenVINS
  • Kimera-VIO
  • ORB-SLAM3
  • DM-VIO
  • AirSLAM
  • OKVIS2-X
  • √VINS

The benchmark uses 98 selected sequences from five public datasets, covering indoor and aggressive aerial flight, underwater navigation, wearable sensing, and legged field operation.

  • EuRoCIndoor aerial flight11 sequences
  • UZH-FPVAggressive flight15 sequences
  • AQUALOCUnderwater navigation17 sequences
  • LaMAriaEgocentric wearable sensing21 sequences
  • GrandTourLegged field robotics34 sequences

Evaluation and complete results

All reported results come from our own reruns and evaluations. We use common trajectory-alignment, validity, and aggregation rules, keeping monocular and stereo configurations separate. We report position and orientation ATE, translational and rotational RPE, and valid reference-path coverage (SR).

Separate computational profiling reports native processing time, CPU utilization, and process memory on Desktop, Jetson AGX Orin, and Jetson Nano, using one fixed input from each dataset. Metric definitions and statistical rules are provided alongside the complete result tables.
