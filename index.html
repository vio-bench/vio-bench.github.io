VIOverse is a cross-domain benchmark of 11 open-source visual–inertial odometry (VIO) systems on 98 selected sequences from five public datasets. We evaluate trajectory error together with valid reference-path coverage and profile processing time, CPU utilization, and process memory on desktop and embedded platforms. All results come from our own experiments and evaluations.

Benchmark design

Monocular and stereo configurations are evaluated separately, with loop closure and global map optimization disabled. Computational profiling uses selected inputs on Desktop, Jetson AGX Orin, and Jetson Nano.
