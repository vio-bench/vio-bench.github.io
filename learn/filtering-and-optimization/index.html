Suggested prior reading: VIO initialization

  1. MSCKF Nullspace Projection

    OpenVINS documentation · Main derivation, followed by Implementation

    Follow the feature-Jacobian projection and its use in the measurement update; then inspect the documented QR implementation.

  2. Null-Space-based Marginalization: Analysis and Algorithm

    Yulin Yang, James Maley, and Guoquan Huang · IROS 2017 · Sections II–III; especially III-C, Equivalence of Two Marginalizations, Lemma 1 (PDF page 3)

    Read the elimination comparison together with its noise and linearization assumptions.

  3. First-Estimate Jacobian Estimators

    OpenVINS documentation · EKF Linearized Error-State System; Linearized System Observability; First Estimate Jacobians

    Follow the documented error-state model, unobservable directions, and choice of Jacobian evaluation points.

  4. Optimization-based VINS: Consistency, Marginalization, and FEJ

    Chuchu Chen, Patrick Geneva, Yuxiang Peng, Woosik Lee, and Guoquan Huang · IROS 2023 · Sections II–IV: Optimization-based VINS; Marginalization and Consistency; Marginalization and FEJ

    Read how the paper formulates the estimation problem and applies FEJ to its four marginalization strategies.

  5. Is Iteration Worth It? Revisit Its Impact in Sliding-Window VIO

    Chuchu Chen, Yuxiang Peng, and Guoquan Huang · ICRA 2025 · Sections III–V: Problem Statement; Estimation Algorithms; Experiments. Begin the comparison with Table I.

    Compare the specified measurement relinearization and processing choices, then read the experimental setup before interpreting the results.
