Visual–inertial odometry tutorials

A tutorial series by the VIOVERSE authors on notation, measurement models, estimation, implementation, and evaluation.

Chapter text is in preparation.

01

Notation

Symbols, coordinate frames, rotation and pose conventions, and state definitions.

02

Camera and IMU measurement models

The visual–inertial state, camera and IMU measurements, and the relationship between odometry and SLAM.

03

Coordinate frames and pose conventions

Coordinate frames, camera–IMU geometry, pose representations, and orientation errors.

04

Camera–IMU calibration and synchronization

Camera and IMU calibration parameters, sensor synchronization, and calibration procedures.

05

VIO initialization

The initialization problem, stationary and dynamic startup, and state refinement.

06

Filtering and optimization

Filtering, optimization, feature elimination, marginalization, observability, and consistency.

07

Trajectory evaluation

Trajectory conventions, association, alignment, error metrics, and evaluation with EPICA.

08

Runtime and platform measurements

Processing time, computational resources, profiling procedures, and platform-dependent measurements.

09

Working with your own sensor data

Sensor preparation, calibration, configuration, data recording, and trajectory export.

References and further reading accompany the tutorial topics. Practical material is available in the implementation guides, system documentation, and dataset and software resources.
