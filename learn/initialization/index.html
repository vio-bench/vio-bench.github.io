VIO initialization

Follow the original material in the order below. Each entry identifies its source, the relevant section, and the topic to study.

Suggested prior reading: Camera and IMU measurement models · Camera–IMU calibration and synchronization

  1. OpenVINS State Initialization: Details and Derivations

    Patrick Geneva and Guoquan Huang · RPNG-2022-INIT, February 2023 revision · Section 1, Introduction

    Read the initialization objective, the discussion of unobservable directions, and the motivation for recovering initial covariance.

  2. StaticInitializer

    OpenVINS documentation · Class description; initialize() function documentation

    Read the standstill assumption, the use of the previous measurement window, and the documented behavior of wait_for_jerk.

  3. DynamicInitializer

    OpenVINS documentation · Class description, three-step initialization outline; initialize() function documentation

    Review the documented sequence of preintegration, constrained initialization, and maximum-likelihood refinement, including its stated bias assumption.

  4. Least-squares Problem

    Patrick Geneva and Guoquan Huang · RPNG-2022-INIT, February 2023 revision · Section 3, Least-squares Problem; subsections 3.1–3.6

    Follow the inertial and bearing models through frame transformation, the linear system, the gravity-magnitude constraint, and recovery of the inertial states. Note the calibration assumptions in Section 3.2.

  5. Maximum Likelihood Estimation

    Patrick Geneva and Guoquan Huang · RPNG-2022-INIT, February 2023 revision · Section 4, Maximum Likelihood Estimation; subsections 4.1–4.3

    Read the inertial measurement model, camera measurement model, and prior cost used in the refinement formulation.
