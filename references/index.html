Frames and transforms

Begin with Inertial State Vector in the OpenVINS propagation derivation, then read Euclidean Transformation in the camera measurement update. These sections define the state frames and camera–IMU geometry used in those derivations.

Corresponding chapter: Coordinate frames and pose conventions.

Quaternions and the IMU state

Read JPL Quaternion Definition and Error State Definition together. The IMU class reference gives the corresponding state definition and update operation. For exported trajectories, consult Filter Error Evaluation Methods, under Collection and Transformation.

Measurement and timing conventions

The OpenVINS IMU Measurements section defines the measurement model and noise terms. Read its IMU Kinematic Equations alongside the IMU Noise Calibration parameter table. The State time-offset parameter documents the camera–IMU offset sign.

Corresponding chapter: Camera–IMU calibration and synchronization.

Estimation and evaluation

For observability and linearization, read the three sections of First-Estimate Jacobian Estimators in order. Continue with Sections II–IV of Optimization-based VINS: Consistency, Marginalization, and FEJ. Corresponding chapter: Filtering and optimization.

The Evaluation guide documents the trajectory comparison workflow and EPICA. For the published result tables, use the benchmark protocol and its source annotations.

Primary references

  1. Guoquan Huang. Visual-Inertial Navigation: A Concise Review. arXiv:1906.02650, 2019.

    Visual–inertial estimation models, algorithm families, observability, and open research problems.

  2. Patrick Geneva, Kevin Eckenhoff, Woosik Lee, Yulin Yang, and Guoquan Huang. OpenVINS: A Research Platform for Visual-Inertial Estimation. IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2020.

    MSCKF state representation, feature representations, calibration, consistency, and the OpenVINS implementation.

  3. Patrick Geneva and Guoquan Huang. OpenVINS State Initialization: Details and Derivations. Technical report RPNG-2022-INIT, February revision, 2023.

    Dynamic initialization, inertial preintegration, gravity-constrained initial estimation, nonlinear refinement, and covariance recovery.

  4. Chuchu Chen, Patrick Geneva, Yuxiang Peng, Woosik Lee, and Guoquan Huang. Optimization-based VINS: Consistency, Marginalization, and FEJ. IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS), 2023.

    Observability, Schur-complement marginalization, and correct first-estimate Jacobian use in optimization-based VINS.

  5. Chuchu Chen, Yuxiang Peng, and Guoquan Huang. Is Iteration Worth It? Revisit Its Impact in Sliding-Window VIO. IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2025.

    Controlled study of camera/IMU relinearization and FEJ in fixed-size sliding-window VIO, without long-term loop closure or global mapping.

  6. Yulin Yang, James Maley, and Guoquan Huang. Null-Space-based Marginalization: Analysis and Algorithm. IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS), 2017.

    Equivalence of nullspace projection and Schur-complement elimination under matched linearizations and the stated Gaussian-noise assumptions.

  7. Yulin Yang, Patrick Geneva, Xingxing Zuo, and Guoquan Huang. Online Self-Calibration for Visual-Inertial Navigation: Models, Analysis, and Degeneracy. IEEE Transactions on Robotics, 2023.

    IMU/camera intrinsic and spatial-temporal calibration, rolling-shutter readout, observability under excitation, and degenerate motions.

  8. Yuxiang Peng, Chuchu Chen, Kejian Wu, and Guoquan Huang. √VINS: Robust and Ultrafast Square-Root Filter-Based 3D Motion Tracking. IEEE Transactions on Robotics, 2025.

    Square-root covariance filtering with an LLT-based update that preserves the triangular factor, plus dynamic initialization.

Additional implementation and dataset sources are linked in the relevant chapters.
